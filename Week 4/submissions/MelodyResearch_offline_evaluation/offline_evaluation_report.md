# Offline evaluation of saved Week 3 Jacobian-lens bias readouts

Generated by `evaluate_saved_week3.py`. Inputs are only the saved public CSVs in `Week 3/results/`. No model was loaded, downloaded or run; no GPU, network or paid compute was used; the source CSVs were not modified (SHA-256 identical before and after).

**Sign convention.** `rank_gap = counter_rank - stereotype_rank`; negative means the counter-stereotype target text ranks stronger than the stereotype target text. Ranks are token ranks of target texts in a readout at the saved analysis position. They are not generated answers, and nothing here says what the model would have generated.

**Units and denominators.** 10 independent base items -> 40 matched MC/open pairs (item x context x polarity) -> 80 evaluations (40 per format). The L15-L22 window has 8 lens rows per evaluation, so 320 rows per format. Each result says which unit it uses. Pair-level counts (k/40) are not 40 independent observations; intervals resample the 10 base items.

### 2.6 Uncertainty

Intervals come from a deterministic cluster bootstrap that resamples the 10 base items with replacement (B=10000, seed=20261020, 95% percentile), plus leave-one-base-item-out (LOBO) ranges. With 10 clusters these intervals are rough and descriptive. They are not hypothesis tests, and no p-values or significance claims are made. A proportion that is 40/40 gets a degenerate interval, and that does not mean it is certain.

## 3. Causal RQ4: still unanswered

Nothing here answers the causal research question (RQ4). No activation patching, ablation, steering or other intervention was run, and none can be run without model inference, which this offline evaluation leaves out on purpose. Every result above is a descriptive or correlational summary of saved token-rank readouts. Agreement between lens and output-layer gaps does not show that internal representations cause output rankings or answer choices.

## Limitations

- There are only 10 base items from one saved Week 3 run of one model. Pair- and evaluation-level counts are clustered within base items.
- Ranks are heavy-tailed and bounded by vocabulary size, so means are dominated by a few very large MC gaps (see 2.5). The medians, trimmed means and log ratios are there for that reason.
- The ranks belong to target-text tokens at one saved analysis position. The way multi-token targets were combined into one rank comes from Week 3 and is not re-checked here. MC prompts may favour answer-letter tokens over answer text, which could inflate MC rank magnitudes. That is a hypothesis, not something tested here.
- The output-layer row is a token-rank readout, not a generated answer or an accuracy measure.
- Summary-table agreement is checked to an absolute tolerance of 0.01 to allow for CSV rounding.
- Zero gaps count as their own sign (np.sign = 0) in all sign comparisons.
